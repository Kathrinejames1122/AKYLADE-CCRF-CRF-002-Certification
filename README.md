
# AKYLADE Certified Cyber Resilience Fundamentals (A/CCRF) Exam Guide

A dedicated study guide and cybersecurity framework reference repository for the **AKYLADE Certified Cyber Resilience Fundamentals (A/CCRF), exam CRF-002**, based on the published NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 objectives.

---

## Exam Overview

The AKYLADE Certified Cyber Resilience Fundamentals certification was designed to validate theoretical knowledge of NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0, including its core functions, implementation tiers, profiles, and organizational risk management. AKYLADE ceased operations on June 30, 2026, and CRF-002 is no longer available to sit.

- **Exam Format**: The archived CRF-002 objectives do not establish a currently available examination format. AKYLADE examinations are no longer offered.
- **Target Audience**: Cybersecurity professionals, risk analysts, information security practitioners, IT managers, and professionals studying organizational cyber resilience.
- **Prerequisites**: No formal prerequisite is specified in the published CRF-002 objectives.

---

## Core Exam Domains & Knowledge Areas

### 1. Framework Concepts

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 purpose, history, terminology, and applicability across industries.
- Framework components: Core, Organizational Profiles, and Implementation Tiers.
- Cyber resilience benefits, framework characteristics, and relationships with other standards.

### 2. Framework Core

- Six functions: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover.
- Framework categories, subcategories, and intended cybersecurity outcomes.
- Mapping NIST CSF outcomes to standards, controls, and informative references.

### 3. Implementation Tiers

- NIST CSF Implementation Tiers and their relationship to organizational cybersecurity risk management.
- Tier characteristics: Partial, Risk Informed, Repeatable, and Adaptive.
- Assessing current cybersecurity posture and planning improvements toward a target tier.

### 4. Framework Profiles

- Profile components, including functions, categories, and subcategories.
- Current and Target Profiles for assessing organizational cybersecurity posture.
- Tailoring profiles to organizational risk strategies, regulatory needs, and sector requirements.

### 5. Risk Management

- Qualitative, quantitative, and hybrid risk analysis methods.
- Risk appetite, risk tolerance, business impact analysis, and risk response options.
- Identifying threats and vulnerabilities, evaluating mitigation strategies, and developing risk management plans.
